## 2026-02-04

🚀 Track your reviews with PostHog

Understand how Fast Review is working for you! You can now send review metrics to PostHog for analysis.

To enable tracking, add your PostHog API key:
```yaml
- uses: tunajam/fast-review@main
  with:
    posthog-api-key: ${{ secrets.POSTHOG_API_KEY }}
```
